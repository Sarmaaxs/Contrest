## About Contrest

Contrest is a social content platform built around the way people create, share and discover things that matter to them. It brings everyday social interaction and creator-focused experiences together in one place, giving people room to publish content, follow conversations and grow their presence.

The goal is simple: make sharing feel natural, discovery feel relevant, and creative expression feel accessible.

## What you can do

- **Share content** — publish and engage with posts from other people.
- **Discover creators** — explore content and find accounts worth following.
- **Connect** — keep up with conversations and communicate with other members.
- **Stay in the loop** — follow activity and updates across your social experience.
- **Create with Studio** — work on content through Contrest's creator-focused experience.

Contrest is an evolving project. Features and availability may change as development continues.
